On-board schedule

Time-tagged commands planned on the ground for the on-board scheduler. Entries can be planned at any time, also during LOS.

Pending changes shows at all times what the next sync will do on board, computed on the ground: entries to insert, and entries deleted or replaced on the ground that are to be removed from board.

Read back reads the on-board schedule and updates the state of the entries from it. Sync reads back, then makes the pending changes on board and verifies them. Both need contact with the spacecraft.

The state of each entry is updated from telemetry, including telemetry recorded on board during LOS and dumped afterwards.
